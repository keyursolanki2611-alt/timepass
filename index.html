What is design thinking?

It is a repeatable method for solving messy problems. Instead of guessing what people want, you watch, ask, and test. A hospital that wants shorter waits might discover the real problem is confusing signs, not slow doctors.

Human-first

Every decision starts with a real person's needs.

Try, don't guess

Cheap prototypes teach you more than long meetings.

Never a straight line

You can go back to any stage when you learn something new.

Case study: Airbnb

In 2009 Airbnb was close to failing. Bookings were flat at about $200 a week. Here is how design thinking changed that.

Empathize

The founders flew to New York and stayed with hosts. They saw that listing photos were dark and blurry, so guests did not trust them.

Define

The problem was not the website. It was that strangers could not judge a home from poor photos.

Ideate

They considered new features, but chose a simple idea: take better photos themselves.

Prototype

They rented a camera and photographed hosts' homes by hand. It did not scale, and that was fine for a first test.

Test

Weekly revenue in those cities doubled. Airbnb later built a professional photography program for hosts.

Lesson

Talking to users in person showed the real problem. Doing something unscalable first was the fastest way to learn.
